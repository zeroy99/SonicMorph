OPEN SOURCE ATTRIBUTION & CREDITS

SONICMORPH is engineered with top-tier open-source audio processing technologies:

Symphonia

MPL-2.0

Pure-Rust audio decoding & media probing engine. Handles demuxing, format detection, and decoding for MP3, WAV, OGG Vorbis, FLAC, and AAC without C/FFI.

github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia

rusty_mp3

Apache-2.0

Pure-Rust MPEG-1/2/2.5 Layer III (MP3) encoder & bit-exact decoder. Enables high-speed CBR audio encoding up to 320 kbps entirely in WebAssembly.

crates.io/crates/rusty_mp3

rusty_vorbis & ogg

Apache-2.0 / MIT

Pure-Rust Ogg Vorbis I encoder and container multiplexer. Delivers native in-browser Ogg Vorbis stream encoding with perceptual Bark psychoacoustics.

crates.io/crates/rusty_vorbis

hound

Apache-2.0

Pure-Rust WAV audio encoding & decoding library supporting 8-bit, 16-bit, 24-bit PCM and 32-bit floating-point audio data.

github.com/ruuda/hound

wasm-bindgen & Serde

MIT / Apache-2.0

High-efficiency JavaScript to WebAssembly bridge and zero-copy serialization for exchanging audio buffers and metadata.

github.com/rustwasm/wasm-bindgen

Web Audio API

W3C Standard

Standardized in-browser real-time audio pipeline and AnalyserNode driving the 60+ FPS live frequency spectrum visualizer and dual A/B playback.
